Why Choose Advanced Anti-Ageing Treatments?

Ageing affects everyone differently. Over time, skin loses elasticity, hydration, and volume, which leads to wrinkles, sagging, and an overall tired appearance. Environmental stressors like sun exposure and pollution only accelerate these effects.

While home care can slow the signs, professional anti-ageing treatments deliver deeper, longer-lasting results by triggering the body's repair processes. These treatments are safe, clinically proven, and tailored to your unique skin needs.

Top 5 Anti-Ageing Treatments at Trivedi Skin Clinic

Here are the top five most sought-after treatments offered by our expert dermatologists that consistently deliver excellent results:

1 Anti-Wrinkle Injections (Botox)

Botox is a widely used non surgical treatment for reducing dynamic wrinkles, including forehead, and frown lines. It works by temporarily relaxing the muscles that cause these wrinkles, resulting in smoother and more rejuvenated skin.

Benefits: Fast, effective, and minimally invasive
Results: Visible in 2–14 days, lasting up to 6 months
Ideal for: Expression lines, early signs of ageing

2 Dermal Fillers

Over time, the face naturally loses volume, leading to sagging, hollow areas, and deep wrinkles. Dermal fillers utilize hyaluronic acid to enhance volume, shape the face, and diminish deeper lines such as smile folds and marionette lines.

Benefits: Immediate lift, non surgical treatment facial reshaping
Results: Last up to 9–18 months
Ideal for: Cheeks, under-eyes, lips, and jawline contouring

3 PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy

This natural treatment involves taking a small blood sample, processing it to extract growth factors, and injecting these into specific facial areas. It stimulates collagen production, improves texture, and restores a youthful glow.

Benefits: Safe, chemical-free, long-term skin rejuvenation
Results: Gradual, cumulative improvement with each session
Ideal for: Fine lines, dullness, and early skin laxity

4 Microcurrent Facials

This advanced facial treatment uses low-level electrical currents to tone and firm facial muscles. It's like a workout for your face, promoting natural lifting and improved skin texture without pain or downtime.

Benefits: Lifts, firms, and improves blood circulation
Results: Cumulative with regular sessions
Ideal for: Sagging skin, early wrinkles, and a tired appearance

5 Skin Tightening with Radiofrequency (RF)

This non-invasive treatment utilizes radiofrequency energy to heat the deeper layers of skin, enhancing collagen and elastin production. It helps tighten loose skin, define facial contours, and improve overall texture.

Benefits: No cuts, no downtime, gradual natural results
Results: Build over 6 sessions
Ideal for: Mild to moderate skin laxity, jawline, and neck tightening

When Should You Start Anti-Ageing Treatments?

The right time to begin depends on your goals. Some start in their 30s or early 40s with preventive care like PRP or microcurrent facials. Others in their 50s or 60s seek stronger correction through Botox or fillers. Regardless of age, consistency and early action give the best long-term results.
