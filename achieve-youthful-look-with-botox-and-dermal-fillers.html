Understanding Botox and Dermal Filler Treatment

Botox therapy involves the injection of small amounts of botulinum toxin into facial muscles, temporarily relaxing them. This process helps soften dynamic wrinkles and lines, particularly around the eyes and forehead. As a non-invasive form of skin treatment, Botox helps maintain facial expressions while minimizing wrinkles and fine lines.

Dermal filler injections, on the other hand, work by replenishing lost volume in areas such as cheeks, lips, and nasolabial folds. These fillers work to smooth wrinkles, define facial contours, and add a youthful fullness. Known as dermal filler treatment, this approach complements Botox therapy by targeting static or structural signs of ageing.
