Dark circles under the eyes are a common concern for individuals of all ages and skin types. They can happen because of reasons like not getting enough sleep, feeling stressed, having a family history of issues, or other health problems. These dark patches can make a person appear tired, older, or unwell. Fortunately, with the right understanding and approach, dark circles can be effectively treated.

At Trivedi Skin Clinic, we offer advanced under eye treatment solutions tailored to your specific skin type and concern. In this blog, we will look at what causes dark circles, clear up common myths, and explore the best treatments for dark circles that are available today.

What Are Dark Circles?

Dark circles are the discoloured areas that appear under the lower eyelids. They can range from bluish or purple hues to brown or black, depending on your skin tone. Often accompanied by puffiness or under-eye bags, dark circles are mostly a cosmetic issue but can sometimes indicate underlying health concerns.

Common Causes of Dark Circles

Identifying the causes of dark circles is the first step toward finding effective solutions. Some common reasons include:

1. Lack of Sleep

Insufficient rest can cause your skin to appear pale, making the blood vessels beneath the surface more visible and creating a darker look.

2. Genetics

Some people are more likely to get dark circles under their eyes because of their genes. Genetic factors can influence pigmentation and the thickness of the skin around the eyes.

3. Ageing

As you age, your skin loses collagen and becomes thinner. This makes blood vessels more visible, causing a shadowed appearance.

4. Dehydration

When the body does not have enough hydration, the skin beneath the eyes may look dull and sunken.

5. Allergies and Rubbing

Itchy eyes caused by allergies may lead to excessive rubbing, which can worsen dark circles and damage delicate skin.

6. Sun Exposure

Too much sun can lead to more melanin, causing dark spots around the eyes.

7. Poor Diet and Lifestyle

Not getting enough nutrients and dealing with high stress can cause dark circles and dull-looking skin under your eyes.

Solutions for Dark Circles

Several solutions exist for addressing dark circles, including home remedies, lifestyle adjustments, and professional treatments. The best solutions for dark circles depend on the cause of the issue and your skin type.

1. Home Remedies and Lifestyle Changes

  • Get at least 7–8 hours of quality sleep.
  • Drink plenty of water throughout the day to stay hydrated.
  • Use cold compresses to reduce puffiness and dark circles.
  • Apply sunscreen regularly to prevent pigmentation.
  • Eat a balanced diet. Make sure you get enough iron and vitamins C and E.

While these changes may help, they often offer only temporary or minimal improvement.

2. Topical Creams

Over-the-counter creams containing ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, retinol, vitamin K, or caffeine may help reduce puffiness and pigmentation over time.
