What Causes Acne?

Before exploring solutions, it's essential to understand the underlying causes of acne. The condition typically results from clogged pores due to:

  • Excess oil (sebum) production
  • Dead skin cell buildup
  • Bacteria on the skin
  • Hormonal changes
  • Stress or poor lifestyle habits
  • Using the wrong skincare or cosmetic products

Each person's skin is different, which is why the causes and severity of acne vary widely.

Types of Acne

Knowing what type of acne you have helps in choosing the correct acne treatment. Common types include:

  • Whiteheads and Blackheads (non-inflammatory)
  • Papules and Pustules (red, inflamed pimples)
  • Nodules and Cysts (severe, deeper lesions that may lead to scarring)

If you have ongoing or painful acne, it’s important to get treatment from a qualified skin specialist.

Simple Tips to Beat Acne Naturally

While professional treatments are effective, everyday habits play a significant role in maintaining clear skin. Here are some dermatologist-approved tips to reduce breakouts:

1. Keep Your Face Clean

Wash your face two times a day with a gentle cleanser that has a balanced pH. Avoid using harsh scrubs because they can irritate your skin and make acne worse.

2. Don't Pick or Pop Pimples

It's tempting, but popping pimples can push bacteria deeper into the skin, thereby increasing inflammation and potentially leading to scarring.

3. Choose the Right Skincare Products

Use non-comedogenic (won't clog pores) and oil-free moisturizers and sunscreens. Avoid heavy makeup or greasy creams.

4. Stay Hydrated and Eat Balanced Meals

Stay hydrated and keep your skin clear by drinking water and eating fruits and vegetables. Eating foods rich in zinc and omega-3 fatty acids is good for your health.

5. Change Pillowcases and Towels Regularly

Dirt and oil from bedding can transfer to your skin, causing breakouts.

6. Manage Stress

Stress hormones can increase oil production, exacerbating acne. Practising yoga, meditation, or regular physical activity can help.
