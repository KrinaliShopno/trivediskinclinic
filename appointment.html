Everything you need to know about slot confirmation, consultation fee, rescheduling, and preparing for your visit.

Instant Response Our dedicated clinic coordinator will call or message you on WhatsApp (+91 99132 48311) within 15 to 30 minutes after you submit the form to verify your chosen slot, confirm Dr. Sunil Trivedi's availability, and send your appointment pass along with the exact Google Map clinic location.

100% Flexible Yes, absolutely! We understand that plans can change. Simply call or WhatsApp us at least 2 hours prior to your scheduled time, and we will happily move your slot to any other date or time that suits your convenience — completely free with zero cancellation charges.

Fixed & Transparent Our standard consultation fee is ₹1,000 for an in-depth, one-on-one diagnostic consultation with Dr. Sunil Trivedi. No advance payment is needed when submitting the form online — you pay directly at the clinic reception or online via UPI/GPay/Card during your consultation.

To help Dr. Trivedi provide the most accurate assessment:
• Current Skincare/Haircare: Bring or take photos of the face washes, serums, creams, or shampoos you currently use.
• Medical History: Any past dermatologist prescriptions, blood tests, or biopsy reports.
• Clean Skin: Try to arrive with minimal or no heavy makeup so your natural skin texture can be evaluated under dermatological lighting.
